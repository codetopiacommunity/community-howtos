# Ask a good question

Stuck? Ask in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel>. The people who answer are
members like you, helping in their free time. A clear question gets an
answer in minutes. A vague one takes hours of back and forth.

Ask about anything tech: code, apps and tools, your laptop or phone,
design, data, hardware, networks, careers in tech, or Codetopia itself.
No question is too basic. Everyone here started as a beginner.

## Step 1: Check if it's already answered

1. If there is a message on your screen, read it to the end. It often
   says what is wrong.
2. Open <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel>. Click the box at the top that
   says **Search or create a post...** and type one word from your
   problem, like *Excel*, *Canva* or *portal*.

   **You will see:** only the posts that match your word. Click a title
   to open that post. If nothing matches, you will see **No matching
   posts**.

   ![The search box with a word typed in, No matching posts and Clear under it, and the Shift + Enter hint](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/02-search.png)

> [!WARNING]
> Do not press <Key>Shift</Key> and <Key>Enter</Key> together in this
> box. That posts your search words as a new question straight away.

3. Found your answer? You are done. Not there? Click **Clear** under the
   box and go on to Step 2.

Asked an AI tool and it didn't help, or you're not sure it's right?
That's fine. Keep what it told you: you will add it in Step 3.

---

## Step 2: Start a new post

1. Click <Button>New Post</Button> at the top right of the channel.

   ![The New Post button at the top right of ask-for-help](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/01-new-post-button.png)

   **You will see:** a form above the posts, with **Title**, **Enter a
   message...** and a row of tags.

2. Click **Title** and type what is wrong, in a few words.

   - Good: *VLOOKUP shows #N/A even though the name is in my list*
   - Good: *Can't download my Canva design as a PDF*
   - Good: *Servo motor not moving with Arduino Uno*
   - Not helpful: *Please help*
   - Not helpful: *It's not working*

   The first time, the channel's **Post Guidelines** may appear under
   the form. They are short and worth reading once.

---

## Step 3: Write your message

1. Click **Enter a message...**. Copy these lines, paste them in, and
   fill in each one:

   ```
   I'm trying to:
   What I did:
   What happened instead:
   What I tried, including AI:
   My setup:
   ```

   *What I tried, including AI* means what you already tried, and what
   any AI tool told you. *My setup* means what you are using, like
   *Excel on Windows 11*, *Canva on my phone* or *Arduino Uno*.

   To start a new line, hold <Key>Shift</Key> and press <Key>Enter</Key>.

2. Got an error message, a formula or code? Paste it **as text**, not
   as a photo of your screen, so people can copy it. Type three
   backticks on the line before it, and three again on the line after
   it:

   ````
   ```
   paste it here
   ```
   ````

   The backtick key is at the top left of most computer keyboards, under
   <Key>Esc</Key> and left of <Key>1</Key>. It is not the quote key next
   to <Key>Enter</Key>. On a phone, look in the symbols keyboard.

   **You will see:** the text between the backticks change to a
   typewriter-style font.

   ![A filled-in post, with the formula between three backticks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/03-title-and-message.png)

   For things you can see, like a design, a page that looks wrong or
   your wiring, add a picture. Use the picture button at the top right
   of the form.

> [!WARNING]
> **Remove passwords, sign-in codes, keys and personal details first,**
> in your text and in your pictures. Everyone in the server can read
> your post. Posted one by mistake? Change it right away, then edit your
> post.

Here is a full example:

````
I'm trying to: show each student's score from my Scores sheet next to their name.
What I did: I typed this formula in B2 and copied it down:
```
=VLOOKUP(A2, Scores!A:B, 2, FALSE)
```
What happened instead: every cell shows #N/A, even for Ama Mensah, who is on both sheets.
What I tried, including AI: I checked the spelling. ChatGPT said to add FALSE at the end. I already had it, and it still shows #N/A.
My setup: Excel (Microsoft 365) on Windows 11.
````

## Step 4: Pick a tag and post

1. In the row of tags, click the one closest to your topic, like
   **Data & AI** or **Design & UX**. New to this? Click **Beginner** too.
   Not sure which fits? Click **General**.

   Do not pick **✅ Solved** yet. That comes at the end.

   ![The tags, with Data & AI picked, and the Post button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/04-tag-and-post.png)

2. Click <Button>Post</Button> at the bottom right of the form.

   **You will see:** your post open on the right, with your name and an
   **OP** label next to it. OP means *original poster*: the person who
   asked.

   ![Your post open on the right, with the formula in a grey box](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/06-your-post.png)

---

## Step 5: While you wait

- Someone will reply when they can. Replies show up inside your post.
- To find your post again, look under <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> in
  the channel list on the left.
- If someone asks you something, answer in your post.
- Keep it in one place. Don't post the same question in other channels,
  and don't message people privately unless they offer.
- No answer after a day? Reply in your post with anything new you have
  tried. That moves it back to the top.

---

## Step 6: Mark it solved

1. Reply in your post with what fixed it. One line is enough.
2. Click **⋯** at the top right of your post, then **Edit Tags**. Scroll
   down the list of tags and tick **✅ Solved**.

   ![The ⋯ menu, with Edit Tags open and Solved ticked](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/07-edit-tags-solved.png)

   **You will see:** **✅ Solved** next to your post's other tags.

   ![The post in the list, with the Solved tag](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/08-solved-tag.png)

3. Thank whoever helped.

**You are done.** The next person with the same problem will find your
answer.

---

## Next, if you like: help someone else

Open a post in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> that you know something
about, and reply.

- Answer in your own words. If AI helped you, check it first, and say
  so.
- Explain why, not just the answer. A hint often helps more.
- Never reply *"just ask ChatGPT"*, and never make fun of a question.
