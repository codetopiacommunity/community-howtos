# Ask a good question

Stuck? Ask in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel>. The people who answer are
members like you, helping in their free time. A clear question gets an
answer in minutes. A vague one takes hours of back and forth.

No question is too basic. Everyone here started as a beginner.

## Step 1: Check if it's already answered

1. Read your error message to the end. The last lines often say what
   is wrong.
2. Open <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel>. Click the box at the top that
   says **Search or create a post...** and type one word from your
   problem, like *npm* or *GitHub*.

   **You will see:** only the posts that match your word. Click a title
   to open that post.

   ![The search box with a word typed in, and the Shift + Enter hint at the right](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/02-search.jpg)

> [!WARNING]
> Do not press <Key>Shift</Key> and <Key>Enter</Key> together in this
> box. That posts your search words as a new question straight away.

3. Found your answer? You are done. Not there? Click **Clear** under the
   box and go on to Step 2.

Asked an AI tool and it didn't help? That's fine. Keep what it told
you: you will add it in Step 3.

---

## Step 2: Start a new post

1. Click <Button>New Post</Button> at the top right of the channel.

   ![The New Post button at the top right of ask-for-help](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/01-new-post-button.jpg)

   **You will see:** a form above the posts, with **Title**, **Enter a
   message...** and a row of tags.

2. Click **Title** and type what is wrong, in a few words.

   - Good: *npm install fails with ENOENT on Windows*
   - Good: *Servo motor not moving with Arduino Uno*
   - Not helpful: *Please help*
   - Not helpful: *It's not working*

   **You will see:** the channel's **Post Guidelines** appear under the
   form. Click **View All** to read them in full.

   ![The Post Guidelines under the form](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/05-guidelines.jpg)

## Step 3: Write your message

1. Click **Enter a message...**. Copy these lines, paste them in, and
   fill in each one:

   ```
   I'm trying to:
   What I did:
   What happened instead:
   What I already tried:
   My setup:
   ```

   *My setup* means your computer and what you are using, like
   *Windows 11, Node 22* or *Arduino Uno, Arduino IDE 2*.

   To start a new line, hold <Key>Shift</Key> and press <Key>Enter</Key>.

2. Paste your error or code **as text**, not as a photo of your screen.
   Type three backticks, <Key>{"`"}</Key><Key>{"`"}</Key><Key>{"`"}</Key>,
   on the line before it and again on the line after it:

   ````
   ```
   paste your error or code here
   ```
   ````

   A backtick is not a quote mark. Its key is usually at the top left of
   a computer keyboard, under <Key>Esc</Key>. On a phone, look in the symbols keyboard.

   **You will see:** the text between the backticks change to a
   typewriter-style font.

   ![A message with an error between three backticks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/03-title-and-message.jpg)

   Use a screenshot only for things you can see, like a page that looks
   wrong. Add it with the picture button at the top right of the form.

> [!WARNING]
> **Remove passwords, API keys and tokens first,** and anything from a
> `.env` file. Everyone in the server can read your post. Posted one by
> mistake? Change that password or key right away, then edit your post.

Here is a full example:

````
I'm trying to: run my first React app.
What I did: I ran npm install in my project folder.
What happened instead:
```
npm ERR! code ENOENT
npm ERR! syscall open
npm ERR! path C:\Users\Ama\my-app\package.json
```
What I already tried: I searched the error. ChatGPT said to reinstall Node, so I did, but I get the same error.
My setup: Windows 11, Node 22.
````

## Step 4: Pick a tag and post

1. In the row of tags, click the one for your topic, like **Software**
   or **Data & AI**. New to coding? Click **Beginner** too. Not sure
   which fits? Click **General**.

   Do not pick **✅ Solved** yet. That comes at the end.

   ![The tags, with General picked, and the Post button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/04-tag-and-post.jpg)

2. Click <Button>Post</Button> at the bottom right of the form.

   **You will see:** your post open on the right, with your name and an
   **OP** label next to it. OP means *original poster*: the person who
   asked.

   ![Your post open on the right, with the error in a grey box](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/06-your-post.jpg)

## Step 5: While you wait

- Someone will reply when they can. Replies show up inside your post.
- To find your post again, look under <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> in
  the channel list on the left.
- If someone asks you something, answer in your post.
- Keep it in one place. Don't post the same question in other channels,
  and don't message people privately unless they offer.
- No answer after a day? Reply in your post with anything new you have
  tried. That moves it back to the top.

---

## Step 6: Mark it solved

1. Reply in your post with what fixed it. One line is enough.
2. Click **⋯** at the top right of your post, then **Edit Tags**. Scroll
   down the list of tags and tick **✅ Solved**.

   ![The ⋯ menu, with Edit Tags open and Solved ticked](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/07-edit-tags-solved.jpg)

   **You will see:** **✅ Solved** under your post's title.

   ![Solved under the post's title](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Getting-Started/ask-a-good-question/08-solved-tag.jpg)

3. Thank whoever helped.

**You are done.** The next person with the same problem will find your
answer.

---

## Next, if you like: help someone else

Open a post in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> that you know something
about, and reply.

- Explain why, not just the fix.
- Be kind. Never make fun of a question.
- Found the answer with an AI tool? Check that it works before you post
  it.
