# Come to an event

Events are the easiest way to meet people here. Most are **X Spaces**:
live audio chats on X that you can listen to from your phone. Now and
then we also run meetups and conferences in person. You do not need to
know anything to come.

New to X Spaces? [Our X Spaces](./x-spaces) shows how to listen and how
to ask to speak.

---

## Step 1: Find an event

Open the
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/events" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">events page</a>.

- Each event shows its **date** and **time**. Times are shown in your
  own time zone, so you do not need to work anything out.
- Use the **Online** or **In person** filter to see only one kind.

New events are also posted in <Channel>announcements</Channel> on Discord. To never
miss one,
[add our events to your calendar](./add-event-to-calendar).

---

## Step 2: Save your spot

If the event has a <Button>Reserve spot</Button> button, click it and fill in the
short form. This tells the organisers you are coming, so they can plan
seats and snacks.

If there is no button, there is nothing to sign up for. Just come.

---

## Step 3: Go

**Online event:** on the day, click <Button>Join session</Button> on the events page.
It opens the meeting in your browser or the meeting app. Join a few
minutes early to check your sound.

**In-person event:** click <Button>View location</Button> to see the place on a map.
Give yourself time to find it.

<DoneWhen>

You have picked an event, saved your spot if it has a
<Button>Reserve spot</Button> button, and know how you will get there:
<Button>Join session</Button> online, or <Button>View location</Button>
in person.

</DoneWhen>

---

## Your first event

- **You do not need to prepare anything.** Come as you are.
- **Coming alone is normal.** Most people do the first time. Say hi to
  whoever is next to you, or to the organisers.
- **Online, you can just listen.** In an X Space nobody sees you, and
  you only speak if you ask to.
- **Everyone follows the
  <a href="https://community.codetopia.org/code-of-conduct" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">code of conduct</a>**
  at events too.

---

## Want to help at an event?

Before an event, the Events team posts a volunteer call in
<Channel>get-involved</Channel>. [Help out](../contributing/help-out) explains how to
sign up.

---

## If something goes wrong

**There are no events on the page.** Nothing is planned yet. New events
are posted in <Channel>announcements</Channel> on Discord, or
[add our events to your calendar](./add-event-to-calendar) so they come
to you.

**Anything else?** Ask in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> on Discord.
Say which event, what you tried and what you saw.
