# Your First Week

You are in. Three small things to do this week, none longer than a few
minutes. You are **not** expected to contribute anything yet.

---

## Step 1: Say hello

1. Open the Codetopia Community server. On a computer, click its icon
   on the far left. On a phone, tap it in the strip on the left of the
   channel list.
2. Under **START HERE**, click or tap **introduce-yourself**.
3. Click or tap the message box at the bottom of the screen.
4. Paste this and fill in the blanks:

   ```
   Hi everyone, I'm ___ from ___.
   I'm here because ___.
   Right now I'm learning ___.
   ```

5. Send it. On a computer, press **Enter**. On a phone, tap the send
   button to the right of the box.

> [!TIP]
> Need a new line without sending? On a computer, hold **Shift** and
> press **Enter**. On a phone, press return.

---

## Step 2: Know four channels

| Channel | Use it for |
|---------|------------|
| **#introduce-yourself** | Saying hello. Once. |
| **#general** | Questions, chat, and asking for help when stuck. |
| **#github-discussions** | Our open source projects. |
| **#rules-and-guidelines** | How we treat each other. |

Ignore every other channel until you are curious.

## Step 3: Reply to one person

1. Go back to **introduce-yourself**.
2. Find someone who posted recently.
3. On a computer, hover over their message and click **Reply**, the
   curved arrow. On a phone, press and hold the message, then tap
   **Reply**.
4. Write one or two sentences. "Welcome, I'm new too" is enough. Send.

## Done when

1. Your hello is in #introduce-yourself
2. You have replied to one person

Stuck? Ask in **#general**. Say what you tried and what happened.
