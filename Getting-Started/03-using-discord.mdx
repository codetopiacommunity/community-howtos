# Using Discord

You are in the server. These are the things you will do most on
Discord. Each one takes a minute to learn.

---

## Post in a forum channel

Some channels, like <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> and <Channel>get-involved</Channel>, are
**forums**. In a forum, every question or request is its own **post**
with a title, and replies stay inside that post, so nothing gets lost.

To start a new post:

1. Open the channel.
2. Click or tap <Button>New Post</Button> at the top of the channel. On a phone, it
   may be a button with a **+**.

   {/* TODO for maintainers: confirm the button label against a
       screenshot on computer and phone. */}
3. Give it a short title that says what it is about, like
   *"Error when I run npm install"*.
4. Add a **tag** if the channel asks for one. Tags are labels that say
   what the post is about, like *Events* or *Career*. Pick the one that
   fits.
5. Write your message underneath and send it.

To reply to someone else's post, open the post by clicking or tapping
its title, then type in the message box at the bottom.

> [!TIP]
> **Pinned** posts sit at the top of a forum with a pin icon. They hold
> things you should always be able to find, like who leads each team.

## Share a win or an idea

Two more forums worth knowing, under **COMMUNITY** in the channel list:

- <Channel>wins-and-milestones</Channel>: got your first job or
  internship, shipped a project, finished a hackathon, passed a
  certification, or gave a talk? Make a new post, pick the tag that fits
  (like *First job* or *Project shipped*), and add a picture if you have
  one. Everyone gets to celebrate with you, and the leads read it when
  they write the monthly recap and choose people for the
  <a href="https://community.codetopia.org/wall-of-impact" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Wall of Impact</a>.
- <Channel>suggestions</Channel>: an idea to make the community better?
  Make a new post with your idea in a few lines.

Big or small, both count. A first pull request is a win too.

## Message someone privately

1. Click or tap the person's name, or their picture, next to any of
   their messages.
2. Click or tap <Button>Message</Button>.
3. Type and send. Only the two of you can see it.

Be kind with private messages: say who you are and why you are writing
in the first message.

No <Button>Message</Button> button? Some people only accept messages from friends.
Mention them in a channel instead, like `@kofi`, or post in the right
channel.

---

## Send a file or a picture

1. Click or tap the **+** button on the left of the message box.
2. Pick the file or picture.
3. Add a message if you like, and send.

---

## Mention someone

To get someone's attention in a channel, type `@` and the start of
their name, then pick them from the list that pops up. They get a
notification. Use it when you need that person, not for everyone.

## Ask a good question

Questions go in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel>, as a new post. You get a faster,
better answer when you include:

1. **What you are trying to do.** *"I'm trying to run my first React
   app."*
2. **What you did.** The steps or command.
3. **What happened instead.** The exact error message, or a screenshot.
4. **What you already tried.**

To paste code or an error so it stays readable, put three backticks
(the key usually under Esc) on the line before and the line after:

````
```
paste your code or error here
```
````

Then wait. Someone will reply when they can. If no one has after a day,
reply to your own post with anything new you found; that brings it back
to the top.

## Calm your notifications

A busy server can buzz all day. You do not have to turn notifications
off; you can choose to be alerted only when someone mentions you.

**For the whole server:**

- **On a phone:** tap the server's name at the top of the channel list,
  tap **Notifications**, and choose <Button>Only @mentions</Button>.
- **On a computer:** right-click the server's icon in the server list,
  click <Button>Notification Settings</Button>, and choose <Button>Only @mentions</Button>.

**For one noisy channel:** press and hold it (phone) or right-click it
(computer), and choose <Button>Mute Channel</Button>.

## Report a message

See a message that breaks our rules, like an insult, spam or anything
that feels wrong? Tell our moderators privately:

- **On a phone:** press and hold the message, tap **Apps**, then
  <Button>Report to moderators</Button>.
- **On a computer:** right-click the message, choose **Apps**, then
  <Button>Report to moderators</Button>.

Add a note if you like, and send. Only the moderators see it, and the
person you reported is not told.
[Staying safe](./staying-safe#report-a-problem) covers private messages
and everything else.

## If your message disappears

**Vector**, our bot, watches the server for two things. Neither means you
are in trouble.

- **A word on our blocked list.** Vector deletes the message straight
  away and sends you a private message saying it was removed for
  containing a banned word. Now and then an ordinary message gets caught
  by mistake. Write it again with different wording.
- **Lots of messages very fast.** If you send 5 messages within 5
  seconds, Vector stops you from sending messages for **10 minutes** and
  tells you in a private message. Wait, then carry on. Next time, put
  everything in one message.

Think a message was removed by mistake? Ask in
<Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> and say what you wrote. A moderator will
look.

> [!NOTE]
> Vector's private messages only reach you if you allow messages from
> server members. If your message vanished and nothing arrived, it was
> most likely one of these two.
