# Our X Spaces

Most of our events are **X Spaces**. A Space is a live audio chat on X
(the app that used to be called Twitter). It works like a radio show you
can join in: a few people talk, everyone else listens, and anyone can
ask to speak.

Nobody sees you. You can listen while you cook or travel, and you only
speak if you want to.

---

## Before you start

You need an **X account**, and you need to be logged in. It is free.

<a href="https://x.com/i/flow/signup" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Create an X account</a>

The **X app** on your phone works best, and you need it to speak. On a
computer, you can listen at
<a href="https://x.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">x.com</a>
in your browser.

**Follow us on X:**
<a href="https://x.com/codetopiacomm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">@codetopiacomm</a>.
When we go live, X shows our Space at the top of your feed.

## Step 1: Find the next Space

Every Space is on the
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/events" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">events page</a>,
marked **Online**, with its date and time in your own time zone. We also
post it in <Channel>announcements</Channel> on Discord and on X when it
starts.

To never miss one,
[add our events to your calendar](./add-event-to-calendar).

## Step 2: Join and listen

1. At the time of the Space, open it on the events page and click
   <Button>JOIN SESSION</Button>. Or tap our Space at the top of your feed
   in the X app.
2. The Space opens. Start listening.
3. You will see who is speaking at the top, and everyone listening below.

## Step 3: Ask to speak (optional)

Have a question, or something to add? You can ask to join in.

1. Tap the **Request** icon: a microphone with a small hand, at the
   bottom of the screen.
2. Wait. The host sees your request and lets you in when there is a
   good moment.
3. When you are let in, tap the **microphone** to unmute, then talk.
4. When you are done, tap the microphone again to mute.

## If something goes wrong

**I can't hear anything.** Turn up your phone's volume, and check it is
not on silent. Then leave the Space and join again.

**There is no Request icon.** Make sure you are logged in to X, and use
the X app on your phone.

**The Space has ended.** Spaces only happen live. Check the
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/events" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">events page</a>
for the next one.

**Anything else?** Ask in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> on Discord.

## Want to speak at a meetup or conference?

For our meetups and conferences, we find speakers through
<a href="https://speakwise.live" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Speakwise</a>,
one of our partners. It is a free website where speakers show their
talks, and where event organisers look for speakers.

If you would like to speak at meetups and conferences, ours or others,
create a free speaker profile there and add your topics and any talks
you have given.
