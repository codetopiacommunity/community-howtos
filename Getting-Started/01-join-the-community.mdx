# Join Codetopia Community

Welcome! We are glad you are here.

Codetopia Community is a place for builders, learners, and creators
figuring it out together. Everyone here started as a beginner, and
nobody is expected to know everything. Want to know more about us
first? Read
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/about" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">About Codetopia Community</a>.

Joining has two parts:

1. **Your portal account** (this guide). The portal is our member
   website. Your account there is your membership card: your profile,
   your teams and your work all live there.
2. **Discord** (the next guide). Discord is the free chat app where the
   community talks every day.

This guide takes about **ten minutes**. There is no rush, and nothing
here is a test.

---

## Before you start

You will need three things:

- A working **email address** that you can open and read right now. If
  you sign up with an email and password, we send you a verification
  link, so you need to be able to get to your inbox.
- Your **correct personal information**. Use your real name and real
  details.
- To be **at least 13 years old**. This is the minimum age for a portal
  account.

> [!WARNING]
> Members who sign up with information that does not belong to them will
> have their accounts removed or banned without notice.

---

## Step 1: Open the portal

Open the sign-up page in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox or
similar):

<a href="https://portal.community.codetopia.org/signup" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">🔗 portal.community.codetopia.org/signup</a>

You will see a form with a **Create Account** button, and under it the
**Google** and **GitHub** buttons.

---

## Step 2: Create your account

There are two ways to sign up. Both end up in the same place.

**Option A: Google or GitHub.** The fastest way: one click, no password
to invent, no email to wait for. Use it if you have a Google account
(you do if you use Gmail or an Android phone) or a
<a href="https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/opensource-onboarding/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md#github" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub</a>
account.

**Option B: Email and password.** You fill in a short form and confirm
your email afterwards.

**Option A:** click the **Google** or **GitHub** button. A Google or GitHub
window opens: pick your account, and if it asks whether Codetopia
Community can use your name and email, say yes. Then jump straight to
Step 4.

**Option B:** fill in the form:

- **First name** and **Last name**, your real ones
- **Email address**, the one you can open right now
- **Username**, the short name everyone sees you as, like `ama-dev`
- **Password**, then the same password again to confirm it

Then click **Create Account**.

### Choosing a username

Your username is how the rest of the community sees you. It becomes
your profile address, like
`portal.community.codetopia.org/@yourusername`.

The rules are simple:

- Lowercase letters, numbers, underscores (`_`) and hyphens (`-`) only
- No capital letters, no spaces, no other symbols
- Up to 50 characters

So `ama-dev`, `kofi_92` and `zainab` all work. `Ama Dev`, `Kofi.92` and
`zainab!` do not.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Choose a username you will still be happy with in three months. Once
> you change it, **you cannot change it again for 90 days**. Pick
> something you would be comfortable introducing yourself as.

### Choosing a password

Your password must be **at least 8 characters**. Longer is better. Use
something you do not use anywhere else, and save it somewhere safe
right now, before you forget it: your phone's password manager, or a
notebook you keep at home.

## Step 3: Verify your email

*Skip this step if you signed up with Google or GitHub.*

We send you an email to check the address is really yours. Open your
inbox and look for one with the subject **Verify your Codetopia
account**. Open it and click the **Verify Email** button.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The verification link **expires after 24 hours**. If you leave it too
> long, it will stop working, and you will need a new one.

If the email is not there:

- **Wait a minute.** It is usually quick, but not always instant.
- **Check your spam or junk folder.** This is where it hides most
  often. In the Gmail app, tap the menu (three lines) at the top left,
  then **Spam**. If you find it there, mark it as "not spam" so future
  emails reach you.
- **Check you typed your email correctly** when signing up.
- **Ask for a new link.** On the portal, click **Resend verification
  email**. Use this if your first link has expired.

> [!TIP]
> You will know this step worked when you can log in to the portal and
> it no longer asks you to verify anything.

## Step 4: Complete onboarding

Once you are in, the portal walks you through a short onboarding. It is
**about ten screens**, and most of them are just reading. Here is the whole
thing up front, so nothing takes you by surprise:

| Screen | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| Terms | Our platform terms and privacy policy |
| Welcome | Who we are as a community |
| Core Values | What we stand for |
| Code of Conduct | How we behave, and how it is enforced |
| Intro Video | A short introduction to Codetopia Community |
| Join Us | Where to find us on Discord and WhatsApp |
| Your Background | Your field (software, design, data and so on) and experience level |
| Your Goals | What you are here to achieve |
| Your Team | Optional: which team you would like to help. You may not see this one |
| Your Profile | Your display name, username, bio (a line or two about you) and links |
| Welcome In | You are all set 🎉 |

A few things worth knowing as you go through:

- **The first five screens are reading.** Actually read them. One is the
  <a href="https://community.codetopia.org/code-of-conduct" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">code of conduct</a>
  (how we treat each other), and you will be asked to agree to it. Agreeing to
  something you have not read is not a great way to start.
- **Your Background, Your Goals and Your Profile ask about you.** Your field, your experience
  level, what you are hoping to get out of the community, and how you
  found us. There are no wrong answers, and nothing here is a test.
- **Answer honestly.** Say you are a beginner if you are a beginner. We
  use these answers to point you at the right teams, projects and
  people. Overstating your experience only gets you matched with work
  that is not a good fit.
- **Your Team is optional**, if you see it. It asks which team you
  would like to help. Most members skip it, and that is fine. You can
  pick one later from your profile.
- **Your Profile is public.** A bio, your skills, and optional
  links to GitHub, LinkedIn, X or your website. You can leave the
  optional parts empty and fill them in later.

> [!TIP]
> The Join Us screen gives you the Discord invite (the link that lets
> you into our server). You do not have to act on it
> right away. Finish onboarding first, then come back here and follow
> the Discord guide properly.

## How to know you are done

You are finished with part one when **all three** of these are true:

1. You can log in to the portal with your email and password, or with
   Google or GitHub
2. You have been through all the onboarding screens
3. You can see your own profile at
   `portal.community.codetopia.org/@yourusername`, with your own
   username in place of `yourusername`

If all three are true, your membership card is ready. 🎉

---

## If something goes wrong

**The verification email never arrived.** See the checklist in Step 3.
Spam folder first, then ask for a new link.

**My verification link says it expired.** Links last 24 hours. Ask the
portal to send a fresh one.

**My username was rejected.** It probably has a capital letter, a
space, or a symbol other than `_` or `-`. It may also already be taken
by another member. Try a variation.

**I forgot my password.** On the login page, click **Forgot password**. That reset link is short-lived, so open it as soon as it arrives
rather than leaving it for later.

**Something else.** You are not stuck on your own. Once you are on
Discord, ask in **#ask-for-help** and someone will help you. Stuck
before you can get into Discord? Email
<a href="mailto:hello@codetopia.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">hello@codetopia.org</a>
and say what you tried and what you saw. Nobody will think less of you
for asking.
