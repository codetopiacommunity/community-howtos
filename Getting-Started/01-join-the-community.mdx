# Join Codetopia Community

Welcome! We are glad you are here.

Codetopia Community is a place for builders, learners, and creators
figuring it out together. We learn in the open, build real things, and
help each other level up. Everyone here started as a beginner, and
nobody is expected to know everything.

This guide takes you from nothing to a finished account, step by step.
It should take about **ten minutes**. Take your time. There is no rush,
and nothing here is a test.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Read these before you join. This part is not optional.
>
> - **<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/about" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">About Codetopia Community</a>**, who we
>   are, what we do, and what we expect from each other. If this is not
>   the kind of place you want to be part of, better to know now than
>   later.
> - **<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/code-of-conduct" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Code of conduct</a>**, how we treat each other
>   here. Every member is held to it from day one, and you will be asked
>   to agree to it during signup. Do not skip it and tick a box you have
>   not read.
>
> Both are short. Read them properly, then come back here and let's get
> you in.

---

## The big picture

Joining happens in two parts:

1. **Create your portal account**, which gives you your identity in the
   community. **This page covers it.**
2. **Join our Discord server**, which is where the community talks every
   day. The next guide covers it.

You are on part one. By the end of this page you will have a working
portal account and you will be ready for Discord.

---

## What is the portal?

The portal is the community's home base. Every member has an account
there. It is your identity in the community, and it is where the real
work happens: you join teams, submit your work for review, and keep
track of everything you are involved in.

Think of it like this: your portal account is your membership card. You
create it once, and it opens every door in the community, including
access to our Discord server.

---

## Before you start

You will need three things:

- A working **email address** that you can open and read right now. If
  you sign up with an email and password, we send you a verification
  link, so you need to be able to get to your inbox.
- Your **correct personal information**. Use your real name and real
  details.
- To be **at least 13 years old**. This is the minimum age for a portal
  account.

> [!WARNING]
> Members who sign up with information that does not belong to them will
> have their accounts removed or banned without notice.

---

## Two ways to sign up

On the signup page you will see two options. Pick whichever suits you.
Neither one is better than the other, and both end up in the same place.

**Option A: Google or GitHub.** One click, no password to invent, and
**no verification email to wait for**. If you already have a Google or
GitHub account, this is the fastest way in.

**Option B: Email and password.** You fill in a short form and confirm
your email address afterwards.

Both options land you at the same onboarding, which is Step 4 below.

> [!TIP]
> If you sign up with Google or GitHub, you can skip Step 3 entirely.
> Your email is already confirmed.

---

## Step 1: Open the portal

Open your browser and go to:

<a href="https://portal.community.codetopia.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">🔗 portal.community.codetopia.org</a>

Look for the option to create a new account, rather than logging in to
an existing one.

---

## Step 2: Create your account

If you chose **Google or GitHub**, click that button, pick your account,
and allow access when your browser asks. Then jump straight to Step 4.

If you chose **email and password**, fill in the form:

- **First name** and **Last name**, your real ones
- **Email address**, the one you can open right now
- **Username**, your handle across the community
- **Password**, then the same password again to confirm it

### Choosing a username

Your username is how the rest of the community sees you. It becomes
your profile address, like
`portal.community.codetopia.org/@yourusername`.

The rules are simple:

- Lowercase letters, numbers, underscores (`_`) and hyphens (`-`) only
- No capital letters, no spaces, no other symbols
- Up to 50 characters

So `ama-dev`, `kofi_92` and `zainab` all work. `Ama Dev`, `Kofi.92` and
`zainab!` do not.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Choose a username you will still be happy with in three months. Once
> you change it, **you cannot change it again for 90 days**. Pick
> something you would be comfortable introducing yourself as.

### Choosing a password

Your password must be **at least 8 characters**. Longer is better. Use
something you do not use anywhere else, and save it somewhere safe
right now, before you forget it.

## Step 3: Verify your email

*Skip this step if you signed up with Google or GitHub.*

We send a verification link to the email address you signed up with.
Open your inbox and click it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The verification link **expires after 24 hours**. If you leave it too
> long, it will stop working, and you will need a new one.

If the email is not there:

- **Wait a minute.** It is usually quick, but not always instant.
- **Check your spam or junk folder.** This is where it hides most
  often. If you find it there, mark it as "not spam" so future emails
  reach you.
- **Check you typed your email correctly** when signing up.
- **Ask for a new link.** The portal can resend it. Use this if your
  first link has expired.

> [!TIP]
> You will know this step worked when you can log in to the portal and
> it no longer asks you to verify anything.

---

## Step 4: Complete onboarding

Once you are in, the portal walks you through a short onboarding. It is
**ten screens**, and most of them are just reading. Here is the whole
thing up front, so nothing takes you by surprise:

| # | Screen | What happens |
|---|--------|--------------|
| 1 | Terms | Our platform terms and privacy policy |
| 2 | Welcome | Who we are as a community |
| 3 | Core Values | What we stand for |
| 4 | Code of Conduct | How we behave, and how it is enforced |
| 5 | Intro Video | A short introduction to Codetopia Community |
| 6 | Join Us | Where to find us on Discord and WhatsApp |
| 7 | Your Background | Your discipline and experience level |
| 8 | Your Goals | What you are here to achieve |
| 9 | Your Profile | Your display name, username, bio and links |
| 10 | Welcome In | You are all set 🎉 |

A few things worth knowing as you go through:

- **Screens 1 to 5 are reading.** Actually read them. Screen 4 is the
  code of conduct, and you will be asked to agree to it. Agreeing to
  something you have not read is not a great way to start.
- **Screens 7 to 9 ask about you.** Your discipline, your experience
  level, what you are hoping to get out of the community, and how you
  found us. There are no wrong answers, and nothing here is a test.
- **Answer honestly.** Say you are a beginner if you are a beginner. We
  use these answers to point you at the right teams, projects and
  people. Overstating your experience only gets you matched with work
  that is not a good fit.
- **Screen 9 is your public profile.** A bio, your skills, and optional
  links to GitHub, LinkedIn, X or your website. You can leave the
  optional parts empty and fill them in later.

> [!TIP]
> Screen 6 gives you the Discord invite. You do not have to act on it
> right away. Finish onboarding first, then come back here and follow
> the Discord guide properly.

## How to know you are done

You are finished with part one when **all three** of these are true:

1. You can log in to the portal with your email and password, or with
   Google or GitHub
2. You have been through all ten onboarding screens
3. You can see your own profile at
   `portal.community.codetopia.org/@yourusername`

If all three are true, your membership card is ready. 🎉

---

## If something goes wrong

**The verification email never arrived.** See the checklist in Step 3.
Spam folder first, then ask for a new link.

**My verification link says it expired.** Links last 24 hours. Ask the
portal to send a fresh one.

**My username was rejected.** It probably has a capital letter, a
space, or a symbol other than `_` or `-`. It may also already be taken
by another member. Try a variation.

**I forgot my password.** Use the password reset option on the login
page. That reset link is short-lived, so open it as soon as it arrives
rather than leaving it for later.

**Something else.** You are not stuck on your own. Once you are on
Discord you can ask in the server, and someone will help you. Nobody
will think less of you for asking.
