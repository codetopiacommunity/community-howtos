# Staying safe

Everyone here deserves to feel safe. Our
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/code-of-conduct" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">code of conduct</a>
says how we treat each other, and our **Community Moderators** make sure
it is followed.

If someone is rude, unkind or makes you uncomfortable, **it is not your
fault, and you do not have to deal with it alone.**

---

## Report a problem

You can report anything that breaks the code of conduct: insults,
harassment, unwanted private messages, spam, or anything that feels
wrong. You do not need to be sure. If in doubt, report it.

**On Discord, report the message to our moderators:**

- **On a phone:** press and hold the message, tap **Apps**, then
  <Button>Report to moderators</Button>.
- **On a computer:** right-click the message, choose **Apps**, then
  <Button>Report to moderators</Button>.

A small form opens. Say what is wrong if you like (you can leave it
empty), and send. Our moderators get the message and your note
privately. The person you reported is not told. When it has been dealt
with, Vector sends you a private message to say so.

**About a private message, or not on Discord?** Email our moderators at
<a href="mailto:codetopiancommunity@gmail.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">codetopiancommunity@gmail.com</a>
(the **Report an incident** button on the
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/code-of-conduct" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">code of conduct page</a>
does the same). Say what happened, when, and who was involved. A
screenshot helps. Reports are handled privately.

**Something illegal or dangerous**, like threats, scams or explicit
content? Also use Discord's own <Button>Report Message</Button> (in the
same menu). That report goes to Discord, which can act across all of
Discord, not only our server.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you are ever in danger, contact someone you trust or the local
> emergency services first.

### Is your report about a moderator or a team lead?

Moderators and team leads follow the same code of conduct as everyone
else. If one of them has treated you or someone else badly, tell us.

This is only for reports about a moderator or a team lead. For
everything else, use <Button>Report to moderators</Button> above.
Messages about anything else will be sent back there.

1. **Message the Community Manager on Discord.** You will find them
   pinned at the top of <Channel>get-involved</Channel>. Click or tap
   their name, then send them a message.
2. **No reply after two days?** Send the same message to the
   **Community Lead**, who is pinned in the same place.

If your report is about the Community Manager, skip step 1 and message
the Community Lead.

In your message, say:

- **what happened**
- **when** it happened
- **who** was involved
- **where**, like the channel or event

Add a screenshot if you have one. You do not need proof to report.

Not sure what to write? Something like this is enough:

```
Hi, I'd like to report something about a moderator, @username.
On 20 October, in #ask-for-help, they replied to my question in a way
that made me feel unwelcome. I've attached a screenshot.
```

Your report is handled privately.

## Block someone

Blocking stops a person from messaging you.

1. Click or tap their name or picture.
2. Open the menu on their profile (the three dots).
3. Choose <Button>Block</Button>.

They are not told that you blocked them.

---

## Protect yourself

- **Never share your passwords,** or the code from a sign-in email,
  with anyone. Nobody from Codetopia will ever ask for them.
- **Be careful with private messages from strangers,** especially ones
  offering jobs, money or prizes, or asking you to click a link. Real
  opportunities are posted publicly on our
  <a href="https://community.codetopia.org/careers" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">careers page</a>.
- **Keep personal details private:** your home address, phone number,
  school timetable. Your profile does not need them.
- **Under 18?** Talk to a parent, guardian or teacher you trust if
  anything online worries you.
