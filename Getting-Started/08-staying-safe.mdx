# Staying safe

Everyone here deserves to feel safe. Our
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/code-of-conduct" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">code of conduct</a>
says how we treat each other, and our **Community Moderators** make sure
it is followed.

If someone is rude, unkind or makes you uncomfortable, **it is not your
fault, and you do not have to deal with it alone.**

---

## Report a problem

You can report anything that breaks the code of conduct: insults,
harassment, unwanted private messages, spam, or anything that feels
wrong. You do not need to be sure. If in doubt, report it.

**On Discord, report the message:**

- **On a phone:** press and hold the message, then tap <Button>Report
  Message</Button>.
- **On a computer:** right-click the message, then click <Button>Report
  Message</Button>.

**Tell our moderators directly:** on the
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/code-of-conduct" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">code of conduct page</a>,
use **Report an incident**, or email
<a href="mailto:codetopiancommunity@gmail.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">codetopiancommunity@gmail.com</a>.
Say what happened, when, and who was involved. A screenshot helps.
Reports are handled privately.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you are ever in danger, contact someone you trust or the local
> emergency services first.

---

## Block someone

Blocking stops a person from messaging you.

1. Click or tap their name or picture.
2. Open the menu on their profile (the three dots).
3. Choose <Button>Block</Button>.

They are not told that you blocked them.

---

## Protect yourself

- **Never share your passwords,** or the code from a sign-in email,
  with anyone. Nobody from Codetopia will ever ask for them.
- **Be careful with private messages from strangers,** especially ones
  offering jobs, money or prizes, or asking you to click a link. Real
  opportunities are posted publicly on our
  <a href="https://community.codetopia.org/careers" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">careers page</a>.
- **Keep personal details private:** your home address, phone number,
  school timetable. Your profile does not need them.
- **Under 18?** Talk to a parent, guardian or teacher you trust if
  anything online worries you.
