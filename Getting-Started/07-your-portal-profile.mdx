# Your portal profile

Your profile on the portal is your page in the community. People see
it when they look you up, and the
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/wall-of-impact" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Wall of Impact</a>
uses your photo from it.

> [!NOTE]
> Your profile is **public**. Anyone can see it at
> `portal.community.codetopia.org/@yourusername`. Only add what you are
> happy for anyone to see.

---

## Edit your profile

1. Open
   <a href="https://portal.community.codetopia.org/settings/profile" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">your profile settings</a>.
   Log in first if the portal asks.
2. Change what you like:
   - **Photo:** click <Button>Upload a photo</Button> and pick a picture of you. A
     clear photo of your face helps people recognise you at events.
   - **Bio:** a line or two about you, like *"CS student learning
     React, into design."*
   - **Skills** and **links:** your GitHub, LinkedIn, X or website, if
     you have them. All optional.
3. Click <Button>Save Changes</Button>.

## Choose a team

If you would like to help one of our teams, you can pick it in your
profile settings, under **Team** (from 14 October 2026, when teams go
live on the portal). The team's lead will see it. Most members leave
it as *No team*.

[Ways to contribute](../contributing/ways-to-contribute) explains what
each team does.

---

## Show your work

Helped a team with something? You can add it to your profile, with a
link to the work. Once a team lead approves it, it shows under
**Contributions** on your public profile.
[Log your work on the portal](../contributing/log-your-work) shows how.

## Change your username

Your username can be changed in your profile settings with
<Button>Edit username</Button>. After you change it, you cannot change it again for
**90 days**, so choose carefully.

## Keep your account safe

Your account settings are on the portal's
<a href="https://portal.community.codetopia.org/settings/security" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Security page</a>.

**Change your password:**

1. Under **Change Password**, type your **Current Password**.
2. Type your **New Password**: at least 8 characters, with at least one
   number. Type it again in **Confirm New Password**.
3. Click <Button>Update Password</Button>. You will see *Password changed
   successfully.*

**Signed up with Google or GitHub?** You do not have a portal password,
and you do not need one: keep using that button to log in. If you ever
need a password (for example to delete your account), log out, click
<Button>Forgot password?</Button> on the login page, and use the link we
email you to set one.

**Lost a phone, or logged in on someone else's computer?** Under **Active
Sessions** you can see every device logged in to your account. Click
<Button>Sign out</Button> next to one, or <Button>Sign Out All Other
Sessions</Button> to log out everywhere except here. Then change your
password.

## Taking a break or leaving

- **Taking a break?** You do not need to tell anyone. Mute the server on
  Discord (see [Using Discord](./using-discord)) and come back whenever
  you like.
- **Leaving for good?** On the portal, go to **Settings**, then
  **Security**, then <Button>Delete Account</Button>. Type your password
  and click <Button>Delete My Account</Button>. This removes your portal
  account and cannot be undone. Signed up with Google or GitHub? Set a
  password first, as in [Keep your account safe](#keep-your-account-safe).
  To leave the Discord server too, open the server menu (tap or click the
  server's name) and choose <Button>Leave Server</Button>.
