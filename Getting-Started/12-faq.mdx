# FAQ

Quick answers to the questions new members ask most. Not here? Ask in
<Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> on Discord.

---

## Is it free?

Yes. Joining Codetopia Community, the events, Discord and these guides
are free.

## Do I need to know how to code?

No. Many members are complete beginners, and many work in design,
writing, data or other fields. Everyone here started as a beginner.

## I'm not in Ghana. Can I join?

Yes. We are based in Ghana and open to the world. Online events and
Discord work from anywhere, and event times show in your own time zone.

## How old do I need to be?

At least **13**. That is the minimum for a portal account, and for
Discord.

## What is the portal, and why do I need it and Discord?

The **portal** is our member website: your account, your profile and
your teams. **Discord** is the chat app where the community talks every
day. Your portal account is what unlocks the Discord server, through
<Command>/link</Command>. See [Join the community](./join-the-community).

## Who is Vector?

Our community bot. It welcomes you, unlocks the server when you type
<Command>/link</Command>, and shows you what to do next when you type <Command>/newhere</Command>. See
[the Discord guide](./discord-guide).

## My message disappeared. Why?

Vector, our bot, deletes messages that contain a blocked word, and stops
anyone who sends lots of messages very fast for 10 minutes. Neither means
you are in trouble. See
[If your message disappears](./using-discord#if-your-message-disappears).

## Can I speak at a Codetopia event?

Yes. In our X Spaces anyone can ask to speak: see
[Our X Spaces](./x-spaces#step-3-ask-to-speak-optional). For meetups and
conferences, we find speakers through
<a href="https://speakwise.live" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Speakwise</a>,
one of our partners. Create a free speaker profile there if you would
like to be considered.

## What is the difference between the Community and Codetopia Academy?

**Codetopia Community** is free: people learning and building together,
events, and helping each other. **Codetopia Academy** runs paid classes
with tutors. For structured courses or mentorship, see
<a href="https://academy.codetopia.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Codetopia Academy</a>.

## Is there a WhatsApp group?

We have a **WhatsApp Channel** for news and announcements. It is one-way:
you can read it, but conversations happen on Discord.

## Is there a newsletter?

Yes. At the bottom of any page on
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">our website</a>,
type your email and click <Button>SUBSCRIBE</Button>. We email you a
link: click it to confirm, or you will not get the newsletter. You do not
need a portal account for it.

## Where do I find jobs and internships?

On our
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/careers" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">careers page</a>.
New roles are also shared in <Channel>announcements</Channel> on Discord.

## How do I get more involved?

Start with [Help out](../contributing/help-out). If you want to do more,
[Ways to contribute](../contributing/ways-to-contribute) explains our
teams and how to join one.

## Someone is making me uncomfortable. What do I do?

Report it to our moderators: press and hold (phone) or right-click
(computer) the message, then **Apps**, then <Button>Report to
moderators</Button>. Only the moderators see it. For private messages,
email codetopiancommunity@gmail.com. See
[Staying safe](./staying-safe#report-a-problem) for more.

## How do I leave?

See [Your portal profile](./your-portal-profile#taking-a-break-or-leaving).
