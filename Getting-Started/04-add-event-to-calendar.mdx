# Add Our Events to Your Calendar

Every meetup, workshop and session we run is on one shared calendar.
Add it to your own Google Calendar once, and new events show up there
on their own.

About **five minutes**, on your phone.

---

## Before you start

You need the **Google Calendar app** on your phone, signed in to your
Google account.

---

## Step 1: Add the calendar

1. Open this link on your phone:

   <a href="https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/embed?src=eventscodetopiacommunity@gmail.com&ctz=Africa/Accra" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">🔗 Codetopia Community calendar</a>

   It opens in your browser.

   ![The Codetopia Community calendar open in a mobile browser](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/mobile-browser.jpg)

2. Tap the **plus sign** at the bottom right of the page.
3. The Google Calendar app opens. Pick the Google account you want to
   add the calendar to.

   ![Choosing a Google account to add the calendar to](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/select-account.jpg)

You will see a message that the calendar was added.

---

## Step 2: Turn on sync

This makes sure new events keep showing up.

1. In the Google Calendar app, tap the **menu icon** (three lines) at
   the top left.

   ![The menu icon at the top left of Google Calendar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/menu-icon.jpg)

2. Scroll down and tap **Settings**.

   ![The Settings button in the Google Calendar menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/settings-button.jpg)

3. Tap **Events Codetopia Community** in the list of calendars.

   ![Events Codetopia Community in the list of calendars](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/codetopia-comm-in-settings.jpg)

4. Make sure the **Sync** switch is on. If it already is, leave it.

   ![The Sync switch turned on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/sync-codetopia-calendar.jpg)

---

## Done when

Our events appear in your calendar next to your own.

Not showing up? Check that Sync is on, then wait a few minutes. Still
nothing? Ask in **#ask-for-help**.
