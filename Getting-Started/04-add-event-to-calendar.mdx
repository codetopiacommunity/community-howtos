# Add Our Events to Your Calendar

Every meetup, workshop and session we run is on one shared calendar.
Add it to your own Google Calendar once, and new events show up there
on their own.

About **five minutes**, on your phone.

---

## Option 1: Google Calendar

### Before you start

You need the **Google Calendar app** on your phone, signed in to your
Google account.

---

## Step 1: Add the calendar

1. Open this link on your phone:

   <a href="https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/embed?src=eventscodetopiacommunity@gmail.com&ctz=Africa/Accra" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">🔗 Codetopia Community calendar</a>

   It opens in your browser.

   ![The Codetopia Community calendar open in a mobile browser](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/mobile-browser.jpg)

2. Tap the **plus sign** at the bottom right of the page.
3. The Google Calendar app opens. Pick the Google account you want to
   add the calendar to.

   ![Choosing a Google account to add the calendar to](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/select-account.jpg)

You will see a message that the calendar was added.

---

## Step 2: Turn on sync

This makes sure new events keep showing up.

1. In the Google Calendar app, tap the **menu icon** (three lines) at
   the top left.

   ![The menu icon at the top left of Google Calendar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/menu-icon.jpg)

2. Scroll down and tap **Settings**.

   ![The Settings button in the Google Calendar menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/settings-button.jpg)

3. Tap **Events Codetopia Community** in the list of calendars.

   ![Events Codetopia Community in the list of calendars](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/codetopia-comm-in-settings.jpg)

4. Make sure the **Sync** switch is on. If it already is, leave it.

   ![The Sync switch turned on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/sync-codetopia-calendar.jpg)

---

## Done when

Our events appear in your calendar next to your own.

Not showing up? Check that Sync is on, then wait a few minutes. Still
nothing? Ask in **#ask-for-help**.

## Option 2: iPhone / Apple Calendar

---

### If you use an iPhone

Prefer the Apple Calendar app? You can subscribe to the Codetopia Community calendar with its iCal link.

**CLick on this link 👇**
<a href="https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/eventscodetopiacommunity@gmail.com/public/basic.ics" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">🔗 Codetopia Community calendar</a>
To copy the address, press and hold the link and tap **Copy Link**.

1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone, scroll down and tap **Calendar**.

    ![Calendar settings](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/calendar-settings.jpg)

2. Tap **Accounts**. This is where your iPhone manages the calendars connected to it.

    ![Calendar accounts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/calendar-account.jpg)

3. Tap **Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar**. 👉 Make sure you pick **Add Subscribed Calendar**. You're subscribing to an existing calendar, so you don't need to create a new one or add an email account.

    ![Add Subscribed Calendar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/subscribed-calendar.jpg)

4. Paste the link you copied into the **Server** field, then tap **Next**.

    ![Paste the calendar link](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/calendar-link.jpg)

5. Check the calendar details, then tap **Save**.

    ![Save the calendar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/save-calendar.jpg)

6. Open the **Calendar** app and tap **Calendars** at the bottom. Make sure **Codetopia Community** is checked so its events show.

    ![Check the calendar is selected](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Getting-Started/event-calendar/calendar-check.jpg)

💡 **What's a subscribed calendar?** It's a calendar you connect to and receive updates from. You don't have to add Codetopia events yourself, because new ones appear in your Apple Calendar automatically.

**✅ You're done!**
Codetopia Community events should now appear in your Apple Calendar.

## If you use a MacBook

You can add the same calendar to the Calendar app on your Mac. Copy the same link from the iPhone section above.

**CLick on this link 👇**
<a href="https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/eventscodetopiacommunity@gmail.com/public/basic.ics" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">🔗 Codetopia Community calendar</a>
To copy the link on a Mac, right-click it and choose **Copy Link Address**.

1. Open the **Calendar** app (search for it with Spotlight, or find it in Applications).
2. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, click **File → New Calendar Subscription**.
3. Paste the link into the field and click **Subscribe**.
4. Choose your settings. You can leave everything at the defaults:
    - **Name**: what the calendar is called on your Mac.
    - **Color**: the color its events use.
    - **Location**: where the calendar is stored.
    - **Auto-refresh**: how often your Mac checks for updates.
5. Click **OK**.
6. Look at the left side of the Calendar app. **Codetopia Community** should be listed under your calendars. If you don't see its events, make sure it's checked.

**✅ You're done!**
The Codetopia Community calendar is now on your Mac.
