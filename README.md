# Start here

You are new. This is step one. It takes about ten minutes.

Codetopia Community is a group of builders and learners figuring it out
together, based in Ghana and open to the world. Everyone here started as
a beginner. These guides take you from "I just found this" to "I made my
first contribution," one small step at a time, and each step tells you
exactly what to do next.

Follow them in order. Do not skip ahead. Nothing here is a test.

## The path

1. **[Join the community](https://community.codetopia.org/howtos/Getting-Started/01-join-the-community)**
   Create your portal account. About ten minutes.
2. **[Discord guide](https://community.codetopia.org/howtos/Getting-Started/02-discord-guide)**
   Get into the server, where the community talks every day.
3. **[Your first week](https://community.codetopia.org/howtos/Getting-Started/03-your-first-week)**
   Where to say hello, which channels matter, and permission to ignore
   the rest.
4. **[Make your first contribution](https://community.codetopia.org/howtos/Contributing/04-make-your-first-contribution)**
   One question, one task, about ten minutes.
5. **[Your first fix](https://community.codetopia.org/howtos/Contributing/05-your-first-fix)**
   Fix one small thing in a real project, entirely in your browser.
6. **[Ways to contribute](https://community.codetopia.org/howtos/Contributing/06-ways-to-contribute)**
   The full menu, once you have won once.

If you get stuck anywhere, ask in the server. Say what you were trying
to do and what you saw instead. Nobody will think less of you.
