# Log your work on the portal

Helped with something? You can add it to your portal profile, with a link
so people can see the work itself. A team lead checks it first. Once they
approve it, it shows on your public profile for anyone to see.

This is **optional**. You are named in the monthly recap whether you log
your work or not, because the leads keep track. Logging it gives you a
lasting record you can point to, like a small portfolio.

About **five minutes** per piece of work.

> [!NOTE]
> This is part of the new team setup on the portal, from **14 October
> 2026**. Before then, the page says *Not available yet*.

---

## What you can log

Anything you did for one of our five teams. For example:

- You designed a flyer for a meetup
- You hosted or helped run a session
- You wrote a blog article
- You took notes or photos at an event

You do not need to be on a team. Any member can log work for any team.

## Code on GitHub is logged for you

Did you make a
<a href="https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/opensource-onboarding/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md#pull-request" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">pull request</a>
to one of our projects on GitHub? You do not need to log it here. When it
is merged (accepted), the portal adds it to your profile on its own.

This only works if your portal profile has the **same GitHub account**
you used for the pull request. To add it:

1. Open
   <a href="https://portal.community.codetopia.org/settings/profile" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">your profile settings</a>.
2. Scroll down to **Social Links**.
3. In the box that says `github.com/username`, type your GitHub username.
   Not sure what it is? On GitHub, click your profile picture at the top
   right, then **Your profile**. It is shown under your name.
4. Click <Button>Save Changes</Button>.

> [!NOTE]
> Signing up to the portal with GitHub does not fill this in for you.
> Add it yourself.

Your merged pull requests then show in **Contribution Activity** on your
portal dashboard and your public profile, marked **PR merged**. A new one
can take up to 15 minutes to appear.

Everything below is for the rest: work that is not a pull request on
GitHub.

## Before you start

You need two things:

1. **Your portal account.** No account yet? Do
   [Join the community](../getting-started/join-the-community) first.
2. **A link to the work.** The work stays where you made it, for example
   in Google Drive, Canva, GitHub or Hashnode. You paste a link to it.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Anyone with the link must be able to open it.** Your lead opens it to
> check your work, and once it is approved, visitors to your profile can
> open it too. If the link is private, nobody can see it.

**To check your link works for other people:** open a private window in
your browser (in Chrome, press <Key>Ctrl</Key> + <Key>Shift</Key> +
<Key>N</Key>, or <Key>⌘</Key> + <Key>Shift</Key> + <Key>N</Key> on a Mac),
paste the link, and see if the work opens. If it asks you to log in or
request access, change who can see it.

**For a Google Doc, Sheet, Slides or Drive file:** open it, click
<Button>Share</Button>, and under **General access** choose **Anyone with
the link**. Then click <Button>Copy link</Button>.

## Step 1: Open your contributions

1. Open
   <a href="https://portal.community.codetopia.org/contributions" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">your contributions</a>.
   Log in first if the portal asks.
2. You will see a page called **Contributions**. If you have not logged
   anything yet, it says *Nothing submitted yet*.

To find this page yourself later: it is in the menu on the left, under
**My Space**. On a phone, tap the menu icon at the top of the page first.

## Step 2: Fill in the form

Click <Button>Submit a contribution</Button> at the top right. A form
opens. Fill in all five boxes:

| Box | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Team** | The team the work was for. Click the box that says *Which team was this for?* and pick one. Not sure? Pick the team that asked you for it, or see the table in [Ways to contribute](./ways-to-contribute#join-a-team). |
| **Title** | A short name for the work, like *Designed the October meetup flyer*. |
| **What you did** | A few lines in your own words. Say what you made and where it was used. |
| **Link to the work** | The link you checked in *Before you start*. It must start with `https://`. |
| **Date done** | The day you finished it. It starts on today's date. Change it if you finished earlier. |

> [!TIP]
> Be specific in **What you did**. *"Made two versions of the flyer, the
> lead picked one, and it was posted on Instagram and in announcements"*
> tells people much more than *"Helped with design"*.

## Step 3: Submit it

Click <Button>Submit for review</Button>.

You will see *Submitted. Your team lead will review it.* at the bottom of
the screen, and your work appears on the page with the label **Awaiting
review**.

It now waits in the review list of the team you picked, on the portal.
That team's lead goes through the list, so you do not need to message
them.

## What happens next

The team's lead opens your link, looks at the work, and marks it with one
of two labels:

- **Approved:** it now shows on your public profile at
  `portal.community.codetopia.org/@yourusername`, under
  **Contributions**, with a link to the work.
- **Declined:** it stays off your public profile. It does not mean you
  did something wrong. Usually the link did not
  open, or the work belongs to a different team.

The lead may leave a short note under your work, with their name in front
of it. Read it: it says why, or what to change.

You are not sent an email when this happens. Open your
<a href="https://portal.community.codetopia.org/contributions" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">contributions</a>
now and then to see the label. Leads review when they can, so this may
take a few days. If it has been
waiting more than a week, message the team's lead. The leads are pinned
at the top of <Channel>get-involved</Channel> on Discord.

## Fix a mistake

You cannot edit work once you have submitted it. Instead:

- **Still marked Awaiting review?** Click <Button>Withdraw</Button> next
  to it, then <Button>Withdraw</Button> again to confirm. It is removed.
  Then submit it again, correctly.
- **Declined?** Fix what the note says, for example make the link open
  for everyone, and submit it again as new.

---

## If something goes wrong

**The form says "Paste a full link starting with https://".** Copy the
whole link from your browser's address bar, including the `https://` at
the start.

**The form says "That date is in the future".** Pick the day you
finished, today or earlier.

**There is no Submit a contribution button, or the page says "Not
available yet".** The new team setup is not switched on yet. Try again
from 14 October 2026.

**Anything else?** Ask in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel> on Discord. Say
what you tried and what you saw.
