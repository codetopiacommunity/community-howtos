# Ways to Contribute

There is no wrong way to be here. Most members show up, learn and help
now and then, and that is exactly what this community is for.

![Join, then show up, then help when you can, then get recognised, then join a team if you want](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/refs/heads/main/images/Contributing/ways-to-contribute.png)

> [!NOTE]
> All roles in the community are voluntary and unpaid, including team
> leads. What you gain is experience, credit, and a public record of
> your work.

---

## Show up

Nothing is expected of you here.

- **Come to events.** Meetups, workshops, online sessions and
  hackathons (events where people build a project together in a day or
  two) are on the
  <a href="https://community.codetopia.org/events" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">events page</a>.
  [Add them to your calendar](../getting-started/add-event-to-calendar)
  so you never miss one.
- **Ask and answer** in **#ask-for-help**.
- **Share what you are building.** Get feedback, find people to build
  with.
- **Learn.** For courses and mentorship, go to
  <a href="https://academy.codetopia.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Codetopia Academy</a>.

## Help when you can

Jump in when a team asks in **#get-involved**, volunteer at an event, or
help someone who is stuck. You are named in the monthly recap for it (a
thank-you post in **#announcements** on Discord, at the end of each
month), and standout work goes on the
<a href="https://community.codetopia.org/wall-of-impact" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Wall of Impact</a>
on our website.

🔗 [Help out](./help-out)

### Write for the blog

Our blog lives on <a href="https://hashnode.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hashnode</a>,
a free website for writing articles. You write in your own Hashnode
account, and the article is published on our blog under your name.

1. No Hashnode account yet?
   <a href="https://hashnode.com/onboard" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Create one</a>,
   for free. Your Hashnode username is shown on your profile, with an
   `@` in front.
2. Post in **#get-involved** with the **Blog** tag: "I'd like to write
   for the blog, my Hashnode is @yourname."
3. Comms & Brand (the team that runs our blog and social media) adds
   you to the blog as a contributor. This happens
   once.
4. Write your article on Hashnode. Until you send it, it is a
   **draft**: saved, but only you can see it. When it is ready, open
   the draft's settings, choose the Codetopia Community blog, and click
   **Submit for review**.
5. An editor (a Comms & Brand member who checks articles) reviews it, then publishes it or leaves comments on what to
   change.

Once it is live, it is shared on our socials and your name goes in the
monthly recap.

### Improve these guides

Spotted a typo, a broken link, or a step that no longer matches what you
see? Anyone can fix it, member or not.

These guides live on GitHub, a website where people keep projects and
work on them together. Never used it? Start with
[Your first pull request](./your-first-pull-request), then see
[Contributing to these guides](https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Join a team

The community is run by five teams of volunteers. Each team has a lead.

| Team | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Events** | Meetups, workshops, online sessions, hackathons |
| **Projects & Tech** | Our <a href="https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/opensource-onboarding/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md#open-source" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">open source</a> projects, the website, Discord bots like Vector, these guides |
| **Comms & Brand** | Social media, design, announcements, the monthly recap |
| **Partnerships** | Sponsors, venues, schools, other communities |
| **Operations** | Tools, records, and the member survey |

Joining a team means a few hours a month for three months. After that,
you choose whether to carry on.

### Want to do more?

Want to join a team, or have something to offer (a workshop, code
reviews, a project idea)? **Post in #get-involved** with the team's tag
and a line about yourself, or message the team's lead privately. The
leads are pinned at the top of #get-involved. Never posted in a forum or
messaged someone on Discord? The
[Using Discord](../getting-started/using-discord)
shows how.

Not sure which team? Ask in **#ask-for-help**.

There is no application form. Most people end up on a team simply by
helping, and a lead asking them.
