# Make Your First Contribution

One question, then one task. Every task here can be finished by someone
on their first day.

## The question

**Do you want to write code, or do something else?**

Not "how good are you." Just what you feel like doing today. Not sure?
Pick **something else**. You can do the other one tomorrow.

---

## I want to code

Fix one typo in a real project, in your browser, nothing to install.
You need a GitHub account. The guide shows you how to get one.

🔗 [Your first fix](./05-your-first-fix)

## I want to do something else

Pick one.

### A. Fresh eyes

Tell us where the guides confused you. Only a new person can see this,
and in two weeks you will not be able to any more.

1. Think back: where did you get stuck? What word meant nothing?
2. Click this link. It opens a form with the title filled in.

   <a href="https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/issues/new?title=Fresh+eyes%3A+what+confused+me+while+joining" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">🔗 Open a fresh-eyes issue</a>

3. Sign in to GitHub if asked. No account? Create one, then click the
   link again.
4. In the big box under the title, type your list. One thing per line.
5. Click the green **Submit new issue** button.

**Done when** the page shows your issue with a number next to the
title.

### B. Welcome someone

1. Open **#introduce-yourself** in Discord.
2. Find three people who posted this week.
3. Reply to each one. Hover and click **Reply** on a computer, press
   and hold then tap **Reply** on a phone.
4. Two sentences each, about what they wrote.

**Done when** three people have a reply from you.

### C. Make or write something

A flyer, a graphic, a short guide. You do not need GitHub. A member
puts it in for you, with your name on it.

1. Make it. Finished beats perfect.
2. Get a link to it. In a Google Doc, click **Share** and allow anyone
   with the link to view.
3. Open **#general** in Discord and post:

   ```
   I made this: [link]
   It's a ___ for ___.
   Can someone help me get it in?
   ```

**Done when** someone replies to say they have it. No reply in a day?
Post it again.
