# Help Out

Helping out means finishing one thing that helps the community. You do
not need permission, a role or a team. Pick something, do it, tell us.

Your first one makes you a **Contributor**: you get the Contributor role
on Discord and your name goes in the monthly recap.

There are three ways to find something to do. Start with whichever
feels easiest.

---

## 1. A starter task

These never close. Every task here can be finished by someone on their
first day.

**Do you want to write code, or do something else?** Not "how good are
you." Just what you feel like doing today. Not sure? Pick **something
else**. You can do the other one tomorrow.

### I want to code

Fix one typo in a real project, in your browser, nothing to install.
You need a GitHub account. The guide shows you how to get one.

🔗 [Your first fix](./05-your-first-fix)

### I want to do something else

Pick one.

#### A. Fresh eyes

Tell us where the guides confused you. Only a new person can see this,
and in two weeks you will not be able to any more.

1. Think back: where did you get stuck? What word meant nothing?
2. Click this link. It opens a form with the title filled in.

   <a href="https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/issues/new?title=Fresh+eyes%3A+what+confused+me+while+joining" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">🔗 Open a fresh-eyes issue</a>

3. Sign in to GitHub if asked. No account? Create one, then click the
   link again.
4. In the big box under the title, type your list. One thing per line.
5. Click the green **Submit new issue** button.

{/* IMAGE NEEDED: images/Contributing/first-contribution/fresh-eyes.png
    Show: the new issue form with the pre-filled title, a few lines in
    the body, and Submit new issue circled. Device: computer. */}

**Done when** the page shows your issue with a number next to the
title.

#### B. Welcome someone

1. Open **#introduce-yourself** in Discord.
2. Find three people who posted this week.
3. Reply to each one. Hover and click **Reply** on a computer, press
   and hold then tap **Reply** on a phone.
4. Two sentences each, about what they wrote.

**Done when** three people have a reply from you.

#### C. Make or write something

A flyer, a graphic, a short guide. You do not need GitHub. A member
puts it in for you, with your name on it.

1. Make it. Finished beats perfect.
2. Get a link to it. In a Google Doc, click **Share** and allow anyone
   with the link to view.
3. Open **#general** in Discord and post:

   ```
   I made this: [link]
   It's a ___ for ___.
   Can someone help me get it in?
   ```

**Done when** someone replies to say they have it. No reply? Post it
again in the same thread. That is expected, not rude.

### Tell us you did it

Post one line in **#general**: `Done: [what you did]`, with a link if
there is one. A lead or core member confirms it and gives you the
Contributor role. No reply? Tag the Community Manager.

## 2. A task from the task board

> [!NOTE]
> The task board opens on 7 November. Until then, the starter tasks
> above are always open.

{/* TODO for maintainers: on 7 November, link the task board forum
    channel here and remove the note above. */}

The task board is a list of things the teams need done. Every task says
which team posted it, how long it takes, the deadline, what "done"
looks like, and who confirms it.

1. Find a task that fits you.
2. Reply **I'm on it** in its thread. First claim wins. No approval
   needed.
3. Do it. Stuck? Ask in the same thread.
4. Post the result in the thread.
5. The person who posted the task confirms it.

**Done when** your task is confirmed.

No word after you post your result? Nudge the person who posted the
task in the thread. Still nothing? Ask the team's lead.

> [!TIP]
> Your claim lapses at the task's deadline. If life gets in the way,
> the task simply reopens for someone else. No hard feelings, and
> nobody chases you.

## 3. Your own idea

Saw something the community needs? Do it, then share it with the team
it belongs to. If it helped, it counts the same as a task from the
board.

Check with the team first only if:

- it will take more than about 2 hours, or
- it speaks for Codetopia in public: it uses the brand, posts on
  official channels, or contacts a partner.

That check saves you from doing work someone else is already doing.

Not sure which team it belongs to?
🔗 [Ways to contribute](./06-ways-to-contribute) lists them all.
