# Help Out

You do not need a role or a team to help. When you have some time, pick
one of these. When you do not, that is fine too.

---

## 1. Jump in when a team asks

Teams post what they need in **#get-involved** on Discord. A flyer for
the next meetup, a hand testing the website, someone to take notes at a
session. Each post says what is needed, by when, and who to talk to.

1. Open **#get-involved**.
2. Find something you can do.
3. Reply in the thread, or message the person who posted it.
4. Do it, and send it to them.

That is all. The person who asked will take it from there.

> [!TIP]
> Nothing there for you right now? Check back later. New asks come up
> every month, and more of them before events.

---

## 2. Volunteer at an event

When an event is coming up, the Events team posts a volunteer call in
**#get-involved** with the **Volunteer call** tag. It is shared on our
socials too.

1. Open the volunteer call and click the form link in it.
2. Fill in the short form: your name, your portal username, the role you
   would like (check-in, setup, photos, timekeeping) and when you are
   free.
3. The Events lead picks volunteers and posts the plan in the volunteer
   call's thread a few days before.
4. Show up and do your part.

> [!NOTE]
> First time volunteering? You will be paired with someone who has done
> it before. You will not be left on your own.

---

## 3. Help someone who is stuck

Questions come into **#ask-for-help** every day. If you know the answer,
or even part of it, reply.

You do not need to be an expert. Someone a few steps behind you is
exactly who you can help. "I had the same error, this fixed it for me"
counts.

---

## How you get recognised

You do not need to report anything. The leads keep track.

- **The monthly recap.** At the end of every month, everyone who helped
  is named, with what they did. It goes out on Discord and our socials.
- **The Wall of Impact.** Standout work can get you a permanent spot on
  the <a href="https://community.codetopia.org/wall-of-impact" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Wall of Impact</a>,
  with your photo and what you did. A lead nominates you.
