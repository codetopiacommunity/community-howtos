# Your first pull request

A real pull request on our practice project, reviewed and merged. You
add one small file about yourself. About **fifteen minutes**, all in your
browser. Nothing to install.

> [!NOTE]
> Words you will see on screen:
>
> - **Repository** (repo): a project's folder of files on GitHub.
> - **Fork**: your own copy of a repo. Changing it cannot break the
>   original.
> - **Commit**: a saved change with a short note.
> - **Pull request** (PR): "here is my change, please review it."
> - **Merge**: accepting a pull request, so the change becomes part of
>   the project.
>
> Want more? The onboarding course's
> <a href="https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/opensource-onboarding/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">glossary</a>
> explains every Git and GitHub word in plain language.

**The plan in one line:** you make your own copy of the project, add
your file to your copy, then ask us to take it into ours.

---

## Before you start

You need a **GitHub account**, and you need to be signed in. You are
signed in when you see your profile picture at the top right of any
GitHub page, not **Sign in** and **Sign up** buttons.

<a href="https://github.com/signup" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Create a GitHub account</a>

Signing up, GitHub asks you to solve a small puzzle to prove you are a
person, and emails you a code to type in. The onboarding
course's
<a href="https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/opensource-onboarding/blob/main/Getting-Started/04-github-account-and-ssh.md" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub account guide</a>
walks through it with pictures.

**Your GitHub username:** click your profile picture at the top right of
any GitHub page. Your username is the name at the very top of the menu
that opens. Write it down: you need it in Step 3.

![The menu under your profile picture, with your username at the top](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/01-username.jpg)

> [!TIP]
> On a phone? GitHub hides some buttons on small screens. Open your
> browser's menu (often three dots) and choose
> <Button>Desktop site</Button>, or use a computer.

## Step 1: Make your own copy (fork)

You cannot change our project directly. Instead, you make your own copy
of it, called a **fork**. You only do this once.

1. Open our practice project:
   <a href="https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/open-source-practice" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">open-source-practice</a>.
2. At the top right, click <Button>Fork</Button>.

   ![The practice project on GitHub, with the Fork button at the top right](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/02-fork-button.jpg)

3. A page called **Create a new fork** opens. Your username is already
   filled in as the **Owner**. Change nothing. Click the green
   <Button>Create fork</Button> button at the bottom.

   ![The Create a new fork page, with the Create fork button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/03-create-fork.jpg)

4. You see *Forking codetopiacommunity/open-source-practice*. Wait a few
   seconds.

You are now in **your copy**. Check the top left of the page: it shows
**your username / open-source-practice**, and under the name it says
*forked from codetopiacommunity/open-source-practice*.

![Your copy of the project, with your username at the top left and "forked from" under the name](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/04-your-copy.jpg)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do Steps 2 to 4 in **your copy**, the one with your username at the
> top left. If it says **codetopiacommunity** there instead, GitHub will
> say *You need to fork this repository to propose changes*. Go back to
> your copy: click your profile picture, then **Repositories**, then
> **open-source-practice**.

---

## Step 2: Open the contributors folder

1. In your copy, click the **contributors** folder.
2. You will see a `README.md` file, and one file for every person who
   has done this before you.
3. Click <Button>Add file</Button> at the top right, then
   <Button>Create new file</Button>.

![The contributors folder in your copy, with the Add file button at the top right](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/05-add-file.jpg)

![The Add file menu, with Create new file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/06-create-new-file.jpg)

---

## Step 3: Write your file

1. In the box that says **Name your file...**, type your **GitHub
   username** followed by `.md`. For example: `kofi-mensah.md`.
2. In the big box below, paste this and fill in the blanks. On a phone,
   press and hold the text to copy it. On a computer, click the copy
   icon at the top right of the box.

   ```
   # Your Name

   - From: Your city, your country
   - Learning right now: what you are learning
   - Fun fact: one thing about you
   ```

   Keep the `#` at the start of the first line: it makes your name a
   heading. Keep the `-` at the start of the other lines too: they make
   a list. Replace everything else with your own words. Three lines is
   plenty.

![The new file editor, with the file name box and the Commit changes button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/07-name-and-write.jpg)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Use your GitHub username as the file name, not your real name. Two
> people can share a name, but never a username.

---

## Step 4: Save it

1. Click the green <Button>Commit changes...</Button> button at the top
   right. A box called **Commit changes** opens.
2. In **Commit message**, replace what is there with what you did:
   `Add kofi-mensah to contributors`, using your username.
3. Leave **Commit directly to the main branch** selected. It only
   changes your copy, so it is safe.
4. Click the green <Button>Commit changes</Button> button.

![The Commit changes box, with the Commit message and the Commit changes button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/08-commit-dialog.jpg)

Your file is now in your copy. Near the top, GitHub says *This branch is
1 commit ahead of codetopiacommunity/open-source-practice:main*. That
means your copy has one change ours does not have yet.

---

## Step 5: Open the pull request

1. Next to *1 commit ahead*, click <Button>Contribute</Button>, then the
   green <Button>Open pull request</Button>.

   ![The Contribute menu, with the Open pull request button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/09-contribute.jpg)

2. A page called **Comparing changes** opens, with *Able to merge* in
   green. The title is already filled in from your commit message.
   Writing a description is optional.
3. Click the green <Button>Create pull request</Button> button.

   ![The pull request form, with the Create pull request button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/10-create-pull-request.jpg)

---

<DoneWhen>

You see your pull request, with a green **Open** label and your
username next to it.

</DoneWhen>

![Your open pull request, with the green Open label](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/11-pull-request-open.jpg)

request's link: copy it from your browser's address bar if you ever need
to share it.

## What happens next

Someone from the Projects & Tech team reviews it. You get an email from
GitHub, and a notification on the bell at the top right of GitHub, each
time they do something.

**Find your pull request again** any time: click the pull request icon
at the top right of any GitHub page. Your pull request is listed there.

![The pull request icon at the top right, and your pull request in the list](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr/12-find-your-pull-request.jpg)

The reviewer does one of two things:

- **Merges it.** The green **Open** label turns into a purple
  **Merged** label. Your file is now in our contributors folder, next to
  everyone else's. You are done.
- **Asks for a small change.** That is normal, not a mistake. Their
  comment is on your pull request page. To make the change: go to
  **your copy**, open the **contributors** folder, click your file, then
  the pencil icon to edit it. Make the change and click
  <Button>Commit changes...</Button>, then <Button>Commit changes</Button>
  again. It joins the same pull request on its own.

## Stuck?

- **No Fork or Add file button?** Make sure you are signed in to
  GitHub. On a phone, switch to the desktop site (see *Before you
  start*).
- **GitHub says "You need to fork this repository to propose
  changes".** You are in our project, not your copy. Go back to your
  copy (Step 1 shows how to tell) and add the file there.
- **GitHub says the file already exists?** Someone already used that
  name. Check that you typed your own username.
- **Already made a copy before?** You only need one. Open it: click
  your profile picture, then **Repositories**, then
  **open-source-practice**, and carry on from Step 2.
- **Anything else?** Ask in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel>. Say what
  you tried and what you saw.
