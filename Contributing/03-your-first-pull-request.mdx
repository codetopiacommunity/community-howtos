# Your first pull request, in your browser

You will add a short file about yourself to our practice project on
GitHub, and we will accept it. About **15 minutes**. You cannot break
anything: the project is made for practice.

Take your time. Everyone here started as a beginner. Stuck at any point?
Ask in <Channel>ask-for-help</Channel>.

---

## Before you start

1. Open <a href="https://github.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">github.com</a>
   (it opens in a new tab). Look at the **top right corner**.

   👀 **If you see Sign in and Sign up**, you need to sign in:

   ![The top right corner when you are not signed in: Sign in and Sign up](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/01-signed-out.jpg)

   Click <Button>Sign in</Button>. Type your GitHub username or email,
   then your password, and click the green <Button>Sign in</Button>.
   No account yet? On that page, click **Create an account**.

   ![The Sign in to GitHub page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/03-sign-in-page.jpg)

   👀 **If you see a small circle**, you are already signed in. The circle
   is your profile picture (a coloured pattern if you never added a
   photo).

   ![The top right corner when you are signed in: a small circle](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/02-signed-in.jpg)

2. Click the **small circle**.

   👀 A menu opens. Your **username** is at the very top. Write it down
   exactly, with the same letters and dashes. Then click anywhere else
   to close the menu.

   ![The menu, with your username at the top](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/04-username.jpg)

## Step 1: Make your own copy

1. Open our practice project:
   <a href="https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/open-source-practice" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">open-source-practice</a>.
2. At the top right, click the word <Button>Fork</Button>. A fork is
   your own copy of the project.

   ![Our practice project, with the Fork button at the top right](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/05-fork.jpg)

   👀 A page called **Create a new fork**.

3. Change nothing. Click the green <Button>Create fork</Button> button.

   ![The Create a new fork page, with the Create fork button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/06-create-fork.jpg)

   👀 *Forking...* for a few seconds, then **your copy**. Under the big
   title it says ***forked from codetopiacommunity/open-source-practice***.
   That line is how you always know you are in your copy.

   ![Your copy, with forked from codetopiacommunity/open-source-practice under the title](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/07-your-copy.jpg)

> [!WARNING]
> **Do you see "No available destinations to fork this repository"?**
> You already made a copy before. Click the link under that message:
> it opens your copy. Then go on to Step 2.
>
> ![The message No available destinations to fork this repository, with the link to your copy](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/08-already-have-a-copy.jpg)

## Step 2: Create your file

1. In your copy, click the **contributors** folder.

   ![Your copy, with the contributors folder](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/09-contributors.jpg)

2. At the top right, click <Button>Add file</Button>, then
   <Button>Create new file</Button>.

   ![The Add file button, and Create new file in its menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/10-add-file.jpg)

   **On a phone**, there is no Add file button. Tap **⋯** at the top
   right, then <Button>Create new file</Button>. Do not tap the red
   **Delete directory**.

   ![On a phone: the ⋯ menu with Create new file at the top](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/11-phone-menu.jpg)

   👀 A small box that says **Name your file...**, and a big empty box
   below it.

   ![The new file page, with the Name your file box and the big empty box](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/12-new-file-page.jpg)

> [!WARNING]
> **Do you see "You need to fork this repository to propose changes"?**
> You are in our project, not your copy: the top left says
> **codetopiacommunity**. Do not click the green button on that page.
> Go to your copy instead: click the **small circle** at the top right,
> then **Repositories**, then **open-source-practice**. Then do Step 2
> again.
>
> ![The page that says You need to fork this repository, with codetopiacommunity at the top left](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/13-wrong-place.jpg)

## Step 3: Write about yourself

1. Click inside the **Name your file...** box. Type your username, then
   `.md` (a dot and the letters m and d), with no spaces. For example:

   ```
   kofi-mensah.md
   ```

2. Copy these lines. On a computer, move your mouse over the grey box:
   a small **copy button** appears at its top right. Click it. On a
   phone, the copy button is always there.

   ```
   # Your Name

   - From: Your city, your country
   - Learning right now: what you are learning
   - Fun fact: one thing about you
   ```

   ![The grey box with the copy button at its top right](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/14-copy-button.jpg)

3. Click inside the big box on GitHub and paste: press <Key>Ctrl</Key>
   and <Key>V</Key> together (<Key>Cmd</Key> and <Key>V</Key> on a
   Mac), or right-click and choose **Paste**. On a phone, press and hold
   inside the box, then tap **Paste**.

4. Change the words to your own. Keep the `#` and the `-` at the start
   of the lines. For example:

   ```
   # Ama Owusu

   - From: Kumasi, Ghana
   - Learning right now: HTML and CSS
   - Fun fact: I can solve a Rubik's cube
   ```

   👀 Your file name in the small box, and your lines in the big box.

   ![The file name and your lines on GitHub](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/15-your-lines.jpg)

   If a line with only a `-` appears at the end, delete it.

---

## Step 4: Save it

1. Click the green <Button>Commit changes...</Button> button at the top
   right. Commit means save.

   👀 A box opens in the middle of the page.

2. Click the green <Button>Commit changes</Button> button at the bottom
   of that box. You can leave everything else as it is.

   ![The Commit changes button at the top, and the Commit changes button in the box](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/16-commit-box.jpg)

   👀 Your file is in the list, and near the top it says *This branch
   is **1 commit ahead** of codetopiacommunity/open-source-practice:main*.

   ![Your file in the list, and 1 commit ahead near the top](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/17-one-commit-ahead.jpg)

> [!WARNING]
> **Do you see "A file with the same name already exists"?** You added
> your file before. Click **Cancel**, then click **contributors** at the
> top of the page, and go on to Step 5.

---

## Step 5: Ask us to take it

1. On the line that says *1 commit ahead*, click
   <Button>Contribute</Button>, then the green
   <Button>Open pull request</Button>. A pull request asks us to take
   your change into our project.

   ![The Contribute button, and Open pull request](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/18-contribute.jpg)

   👀 A page called **Comparing changes**, with **Able to merge** in
   green.

2. Change nothing. Click the green <Button>Create pull request</Button>
   button.

   ![Able to merge, and the Create pull request button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/19-create-pull-request.jpg)

   👀 A green **Open** label at the top. That is your pull request.

   ![Your pull request, with the green Open label](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/20-open.jpg)

🎉 **You did it.** You made your first pull request. There is nothing
else you need to do right now.

## What happens next

Someone from our team looks at your pull request. When they do, GitHub
sends you an email, and a **blue dot** appears on the **inbox icon** at
the top right of GitHub. Click it to see what happened.

![The inbox icon with a blue dot, and the notification](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/21-notification.jpg)

**A purple Merged label** means we accepted it. Your file is now in our
project. Share it in <Channel>wins-and-milestones</Channel>!

![Your pull request with the purple Merged label](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/22-merged.jpg)

**If we ask for a change**, that is normal. Go to your copy (the small
circle, then **Repositories**, then **open-source-practice**), click
**contributors**, then your file. Click the **pencil** at the top right
of your file.

![Your file, with the pencil at the top right](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/23-pencil.jpg)

Make the change, then click <Button>Commit changes...</Button> and
<Button>Commit changes</Button> again, as in Step 4. Your change joins
the same pull request on its own.

![Your file open for editing, with the Commit changes button](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/main/images/Contributing/first-pr-v2/24-edit-and-save.jpg)

**Nobody has replied after 3 days?** Ask in
<Channel>ask-for-help</Channel>, and paste the address of your pull
request's page.
