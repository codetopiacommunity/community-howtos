# Ways to Contribute

There is no wrong way to be here. Most members show up, learn and help
now and then, and that is exactly what this community is for.

```
Join → Show up → Help when you can → Get recognised → Join a team (if you want)
```

> [!NOTE]
> All roles in the community are voluntary and unpaid, including team
> leads. What you gain is experience, credit, and a public record of
> your work.

---

## Show up

Nothing is expected of you here.

- **Come to events.** Meetups, workshops, online sessions and
  hackathons are on the
  <a href="https://community.codetopia.org/events" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">events page</a>.
  [Add them to your calendar](../Getting-Started/04-add-event-to-calendar)
  so you never miss one.
- **Ask and answer** in **#ask-for-help**.
- **Share what you are building.** Get feedback, find people to build
  with.
- **Learn.** For courses and mentorship, go to
  <a href="https://academy.codetopia.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Codetopia Academy</a>.

---

## Help when you can

Jump in when a team asks in **#get-involved**, volunteer at an event, or
help someone who is stuck. You are named in the monthly recap for it,
and standout work goes on the Wall of Impact.

🔗 [Help out](./04-make-your-first-contribution)

### Write for the blog

Want to publish an article on Codetopia Community's blog? Write your
draft anywhere, then send it to the Comms & Brand team on Discord. They
will edit it and publish it under your name.

### Improve these guides

Spotted a typo, a broken link, or a step that no longer matches what you
see? Anyone can fix it, member or not.

🔗 [Contributing to these guides](https://github.com/codetopiacommunity/community-howtos/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)

## Join a team

The community is run by six teams of volunteers. Each team has a lead.

| Team | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Events** | Meetups, workshops, online sessions, hackathons |
| **Projects & Tech** | Our open source projects, the website, Discord bots |
| **Guides** | These guides and the community docs |
| **Comms & Brand** | Social media, design, announcements, the monthly recap |
| **Partnerships** | Sponsors, venues, schools, other communities |
| **Operations** | Tools, records, and the member survey |

There is no application form. There are two ways in:

- **Keep helping.** Leads notice the people who show up, and they will
  ask you.
- **Tell a lead.** Message the lead of the team you are interested in,
  or ask in **#ask-for-help** who to talk to.

Joining a team means a few hours a month for three months. After that,
you choose whether to carry on.
